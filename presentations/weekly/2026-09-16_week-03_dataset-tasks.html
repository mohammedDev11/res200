Audio & Speech Tasks Explored

Audio research does not have one fixed task taxonomy. Modern benchmarks cover a broad range of speech, sound, voice, music, and reasoning capabilities.

Speech Content

  • Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)
  • Speech-to-Text
  • Keyword / Spoken-Term Detection
  • Speech Command Recognition
  • Language Identification

Speaker & Voice

  • Speaker Identification
  • Speaker Verification
  • Speaker Diarization
  • Speaker Counting
  • Gender Recognition
  • Age Recognition
  • Accent Classification

Paralinguistics

  • Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)
  • Stress Detection
  • Sarcasm Detection
  • Voice / Personality Characteristics

Audio Scene & Events

  • Environmental Sound Classification
  • Sound Event Detection
  • Audio Tagging
  • Acoustic Scene Classification
  • Bird / Animal Sound Detection

Audio Understanding

  • Audio Question Answering (AQA)
  • Audio Captioning
  • Audio-Text Matching
  • Audio Classification

Spoken Language Understanding

  • Spoken Question Answering (SQA)
  • Spoken Reading Comprehension
  • Intent Classification
  • Dialogue Act Classification

Audio Reasoning

  • Temporal Reasoning
  • Acoustic Source Inference
  • Spatial Audio Reasoning
  • Multi-Audio Reasoning
  • Audio Question Unanswerability

Music Understanding

  • Music Classification
  • Genre Recognition
  • Instrument Recognition
  • Chord Classification
  • Music Question Answering

Sources: Dynamic-SUPERB, AudioBench, MMAU, Dynamic-SUPERB Phase 2. Full citations appear on Slide 8.

Audio QA Benchmark

AQUA-Bench

Main Task: Audio Question Unanswerability Assessment

Does the model know when the audio does NOT contain enough information to answer?

AADAbsent Answer Detection
IASDIncompatible Answer Set Detection

Audio: cow mooing
Question: “What animal is heard?”   Options: Dog / Cat / Bird / Frog / None of the above

Diagnostic Audio Question Answering

Diagnostic Audio Question Answering (DAQA)

Main Tasks: AQA + Temporal Reasoning

DAQA tests whether a model can reason about temporal relationships between natural sound events. Questions and answers are programmatically generated.

Door closes
→
Dog barks
→
Glass breaks
Question: “What happened after the dog barked?”
Answer: “Glass breaking”
